## Iron-nitride magnets: a loudspeaker, not a motor

Iron nitride is iron and nitrogen locked into a crystal called Fe16N2, and its physics fixes exactly what it can be. The material has enormous magnetisation - more raw magnetic flux than neodymium - but modest anisotropy, so its coercivity (the resistance to being demagnetised) is low. Niron's first-generation magnet delivers about 10 MGOe of energy product, ~0.9 T remanence and 4,000-5,000 Oe of coercivity - https://www.emobility-engineering.com/clean-earth-iron-nitride-magnets/. A neodymium traction magnet needs twelve thousand or more. That places iron nitride precisely: above ferrite, level with bonded neodymium, far below the sintered neodymium a drive motor uses. The often-quoted "up to 36 MGOe" is a second-generation target, not a shipped spec, and no independent lab has verified it - the best Fe16N2 foil ever made reached 20 MGOe but only 1,910 Oe - https://www.nature.com/articles/srep25436.
We went in asking whether a rare-earth-free magnet is a financial-first buy for a real motor; we now believe it is not, through 2028, and the reason is a price the physics forces. On a dollars-per-unit-of-energy basis the mainstream magnets cluster, and sintered neodymium is actually the cheapest energy per unit despite the highest price per kilo - which is why it is hard to dislodge.

So the financial-first test is not "beat ferrite" - it is deliver ~10 MGOe under bonded neodymium's ~$12/kg while being China-free. The feedstock, iron and nitrogen, is cents per kilo, but the processing - nitriding and aligned pressing - dominates the cost, and at a pilot line making just over five tonnes a year that processing is expensive. Niron has never disclosed a delivered price per kilo or a gross margin, so the standalone case is unproven. It rests entirely on a 10,000-tonne-a-year gigafactory planned for 2028 - https://nironmagnetics.com/niron-magnetics-advances-u-s-permanent-magnet-manufacturing-plans/, after the 1,500-tonne Sartell, Minnesota plant opens in 2027.
Then the market voted, in the same week, and it agreed with the physics. Niron's first commercial product, shipping now, is a rare-earth-free loudspeaker magnet - https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/a-loudspeaker-a-magnet-and-a-big-question-can-nirons-iron-nitride-challenge-rare-earth-dominance/ (Scan-Speak and YG Acoustics, plus a FaitalPRO pro-audio design), a ferrite-and-alnico stronghold. Its motor engagements are still labelled "develop" agreements. When a company's first real product lands exactly where the physics says it should, the thesis is confirming itself.
Iron nitride never used dysprosium or terbium. The heavy rare-earth premium it is sold on was never its cost to save.The tell of the whole trade
The window is contested from both sides before iron nitride even qualifies. From above, Proterial's heavy-rare-earth-free NdFeB - https://www.proterial.com/e/press/2025/n0722b.html (Br 1.42 T, no dysprosium or terbium) is full traction performance with a mass-production plant sampling from April 2026. Within iron nitride's own band, Japanese incumbents already ship China-free: Nichia's anisotropic SmFeN reaches 14-18 MGOe - https://www.nichia.co.jp/en/product/magnet.html into motor rotors, and Daido is scaling the same material to 200 tonnes a year. Iron nitride's differentiator narrows from performance to delivered price plus American feedstock security.
And the premium it is marketed on flows to neodymium, in binding tons. Every 2026 magnet supply contract is for NdFeB: Noveon's recycled magnets - https://noveon.co/news/ hold take-or-pay with Nidec, GM and ABB plus a $215M Series C; MP Materials - https://mpmaterials.com/news/mp-materials-selects-northlake-texas-as-the-site-of-10X-a-new-us-rare-earth-magnet-manufacturing-campus/ has a Defense Department $110/kg price floor and a $1.25B Texas magnet campus. The recyclers and miners of the incumbent chemistry get take-or-pay; the rare-earth-free challenger gets venture equity and ships speakers. Even Niron's marquee ~$500M raise is a reported plan, not closed - https://www.axios.com/pro/climate-deals/2026/03/30/niron-magnetics-rare-earth-permanent-magnets.
Verdict · WEAKENED (strong form) → refined thesis CONFIRMEDRare-earth-free magnet chemistry is not a bankable motor play on the China premium through 2028 (strong form 32% → 30%). It banks as a ferrite/audio-class replacement - moat = process IP + Western manufacturing - and its first commercial product proves it (refined 80% → 82%).
The "rare-earth crisis" trade has almost nothing to do with rare-earth-free magnets. Both camps are wrong: the techno-optimists price Niron as an NdFeB-killer; the dismissers call the loudspeaker a lab toy. The loudspeaker is the correct product - iron nitride's low-but-stable magnetics make it a genuinely good rare-earth-free ferrite replacement and a physical non-starter for a traction rotor at once. Use this as a sourcing filter: when a founder pitches a rare-earth-free motor magnet on the supply premium, ask for the delivered $/kg and the qualified design-in date. If both are missing, it is a hedge, not a default.
The Wide Lens · State of Climate Tech
AI's binding constraint is now power, and the response split two ways. Capital poured into firm clean power - Quaise's $134M superhot-rock geothermal - https://www.energyconnects.com/news/renewables/2026/july/geothermal-startup-raises-134-million-for-drilling-superhot-rocks/ with a hyperscaler pre-buying 50 MW, Proxima Fusion's €411M - https://www.proximafusion.com/press-news/proxima-fusion-raises-eu411-million-to-build-europes-commercial-fusion-champion - while New York became the first US state to freeze new hyperscale data centres - https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/first-statewide-moratorium-new-hyperscale-data-centers-launched-governor-kathy-hochul. Demand is pulling firm power to FID; the backlash trails one week behind.
Sodium-ion crossed from validation to grid-scale manufacturing. CATL will deploy 5 GWh of sodium grid batteries across Europe - https://electrek.co/2026/07/16/catl-sodium-ion-15000-cycle-grid-storage/ and Peak Energy is building the first US sodium grid factory - https://sodiumbatteryhub.com/2026/07/09/sodium-ion-battery-plant-coming-to-sacramento/ - non-lithium winning on siting and supply security, not price.
Green hydrogen is supply-ready and demand-starved. The IEA's 2026 review - https://renewablewatch.in/2026/07/17/global-hydrogen-review-2026-report/ shows output up 20% but the 2030 pipeline shrank to 27 Mt as buyers refuse to sign - the same offtake gate that recurs in aviation fuel and carbon removal.
Europe is pivoting from pricing carbon to subsidising industry. The new ETS reform (90%-by-2040) - https://carbonherald.com/eu-targets-90-emissions-cut-with-long-awaited-ets-reform/ ring-fences over half of carbon revenue - a ~€260bn pool - for industrial decarbonisation, up from a tenth. Every EU green-steel case is being re-based on subsidy.
Deep-tech science worth banking. A Nature paper reframes how lithium dendrites fracture solid electrolytes - https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10415-9, and a Joule perovskite triple-junction hit 27.3% - https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joule.2026.102575 - both chip at walls this newsletter has tracked in storage and solar.
The long view: the transition's binding constraint has moved from cost to delivery - power availability, grid lead times and offtake certainty now gate more projects than levelised cost. Over the next 12-36 months the winners solve a deployment bottleneck, not the next cost record.
Placed on the whole transition, magnets are the same story as sodium anodes and green-steel offtake: a China-dependency that does not close but relocates and gets priced. The bankable answer is Western-manufactured, contract-anchored incumbent chemistry (recycled and mined NdFeB); the rare-earth-free magnet is a narrower, later, segment-specific hedge in the good-enough band.
Contrarian Take
When buyers sign take-or-pay for the incumbent chemistry and options-premium equity for the challenger, the challenger is a hedge, not a default - and its own first product agrees. The most investable rare-earth-free exposure for the good-enough motor band may not be iron nitride at all, but Japanese SmFeN (Nichia, Daido), which already ships China-free at higher energy product.
