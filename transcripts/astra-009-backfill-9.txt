The record kept climbing (34.85 to 35.5%); the count of hard bankability gates that fell held at 0 of 4. Records track a press-release cadence, bankability a document cadence, and the gap widened.
A perovskite-silicon tandem stacks two absorbers so each catches the light the other wastes, and that is why it beats plain silicon on physics. Silicon is Auger-limited to about 29.4% (an intrinsic recombination ceiling a single junction cannot pass). A tandem lays a perovskite top cell - a soft, tunable crystal set to a wide ~1.68 eV bandgap so it absorbs blue and green light - monolithically on the silicon below, which then harvests the red and infrared that slips through. Because each photon converts with less heat loss, the theoretical ceiling lifts toward roughly 43% - https://www.nrel.gov/pv/tandems.html. The device works: LONGi's 35.5% certified cell - https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/07/15/longi-sets-new-world-record-with-35-5-efficient-perovskite-silicon-tandem-cell/ sits comfortably above anything silicon alone can ever reach.
We went in asking whether tandem has crossed into a bankable commercial product; we now believe efficiency is banked but a bankable utility module is a 2027-29 event, at slightly lower confidence, because the same soft ionic perovskite that makes it efficient is why it degrades. The wide-bandgap absorber is a mobile ionic crystal: mixed iodide and bromide photo-segregate under illumination (the Hoke effect), moisture hydrolyses it, and heat volatilises the organic cation. The efficiency clause is settled and decisive. The bankability clause is gated by durability certainty, which moves on a different clock and did not move this month.
The single most decisive fact this session is a non-event: the record advanced while zero underwriting documents appeared. The 2026 PVEL reliability scorecard - https://scorecard.pvel.com/ still lists no tandem; no insurer has wrapped a tandem plant; no plant carries non-recourse debt; and Oxford PV - https://www.oxfordpv.com/, the reference Western player, remains at a ~10-year warranty with a 20-year target not before 2028. LONGi announcing a record while disavowing any mass-production plan is the illustration in one line: the frontier that moved is not the one that gates finance.

The one warranty event this year is niche, self-underwritten, and contradicted by the only real field data. The first 25-year tandem warranty came from Microquanta - https://www.microquanta.com/en/ on 24 June, but it is a coloured building-facade (BIPV) product, four-terminal rather than monolithic, and warranted by the maker itself. Set it against the only real 2026 outdoor tandem data, from TNO and Fraunhofer - https://www.tno.nl/en/, and the gap is stark: the module was down to 80% of initial power at five months and half its output by seven, via encapsulation delamination rather than absorber death. LCOE hit if a bank credits 10-yr life, not 25
Where tandem banks first is area-constrained surfaces, not open land, and the LCOE math forces it. LCOE ("the levelised cost of one megawatt-hour over a plant's life") turns on credited lifetime far more than on module price or efficiency: crediting a 10-year rather than a 25-year life more than doubles it, while a 20% module-price cut moves it only about 11%. Run the premium against a real 2026 TOPCon silicon module at ~$0.18/W and tandem loses on cheap open land at every efficiency it can ship. It reaches parity only where roof area is the binding constraint, because there every extra percent of efficiency per square metre is directly extra revenue.
On open utility land the premium runs +33% to +13% even at module dollar-per-watt parity. On area-capped rooftop it collapses to +1.1%. That is the whole area-constrained-first case in one chart.

The first bankable home confirms itself by where the warranty and the orders are landing. The 25-year warranty landing in a facade, Trina's - https://www.trinasolar.com/en-glob first order being premium residential in New Zealand, GCL's - https://www.gclsi.com/ space-grade cells exceeding 30% for satellites, and Swift Solar's - https://www.swiftsolar.com/ traction being US defence microgrids all point to the same place: mass-constrained niches where efficiency-per-area is itself the revenue. The honest counter-pull: the single largest real order, GCL's 1.2 MW to Huaneng, is a ground-mount demonstration with no disclosed dollar-per-watt premium. Direction confirmed, price still unproven.
The US "wall" is real, but it is a manufacturing and IP wall, not a bankability wall. The 45X manufacturing credit survived the July budget, IRS added anti-China (FEOC) conditions, and First Solar - https://www.firstsolar.com/ is pouring the wall with a USITC Section 337 exclusion action naming 47 China-linked TOPCon makers plus an Oxford PV license. But the deployment leg lost its subsidy when the 48E and 45Y credits hit a begin-construction cliff on 4 July, and no US player is near a bankable plant above 100 MW: Tandem PV - https://www.tandempv.com/ runs a 40 MW demo, Swift is pre-financing, Caelux is a 2027 story. A subsidised-manufacturing seat, not a financeable franchise.
Durability is now an engineering problem, not a physics wall, but the proof is still at centimetre scale. The self-assembled-monolayer (SAM) contact chemistry that lifted efficiency is now being turned on stability: HZB's graphene-oxide triple-junction held 27.3% with T90 over 770 hours - https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/07/13/triple-junction-all-perovskite-solar-cell-based-on-graphene-oxide-bilayer-achieves-27-3-efficiency/, and a cross-linked SAM tandem kept above 34% with under 4% loss over 1,200 hours at 65C. Every one of these is a small-area accelerated test. No 2026 result validates Hoke-effect suppression at module scale under real field light - the single open gap that dates the utility inflection to 2027-29.
The UK invents and licenses; Asia deploys - so the durable Western rent is upstream IP and niches, never the gigawatt. Oxford PV - https://www.oxfordpv.com/ monetises more than 400 patents by licensing both Trina (China) and First Solar (US) while building no gigawatts of its own, and the Cambridge and Oxford spinouts around Sam Stranks - https://www.stranks-group.com/ and Henry Snaith - https://www2.physics.ox.ac.uk/contacts/people/snaith (Swift Solar, Clarity Sensors, Power Roll - https://powerroll.solar/) sit in defence, sensing and flexible film. China owns the volume: 75.85% of the patents, the cell records, and the first commercial orders.

Efficiency is banked; a bankable-utility module is a 2027-29 warranty-document event; the rent is upstream IP plus area-constrained niches, never the gigawatt.
In any deep-tech hardware pitch, ask which frontier the founder sells on. If they lead with a performance record, ask for the bankability documents: warranty term, reliability-scorecard entry, insurer guarantee, project debt. That one question separates a science project from a business, and you can ask it without being an engineer. For perovskite specifically: do not underwrite tandem as a utility module in 2026. The first bankable home is area-constrained (facades, premium rooftop, solar-EV, space and defence), and the durable rent is the licensable deposition and encapsulation IP, not the gigawatt.

Engineered carbon removal booked its first North American commercial credits. The long view: the transition's binding constraint has moved from cost to delivery - power availability, transmission lead times (now a $1.1T US number), binding offtake and China-free supply gate more projects than levelised cost. Over 12-36 months the winners solve a deployment bottleneck, and in hardware that bottleneck is durability and warranty certainty, exactly the frontier tandem has not yet crossed.
Placed on the whole transition, tandem is the same story as sodium storage and green hydrogen: the technical merit is real, and a document, not a device, decides when it deploys. Sodium wins on siting and supply security once cells qualify; hydrogen stalls on offtake signatures; tandem waits on a warranty. In each the binding constraint sits one layer past the headline metric, and the capital that reads that layer early is the capital that is not overpaying for the record.
Contrarian Take
Everyone watches the 35%+ efficiency race, but neither efficiency nor dollar-per-watt is the bankability gate - credited lifetime is, and it dominates LCOE about 13 times more than a big module-price cut. The re-rate will be triggered by an unglamorous document (first 20-year warranty, first scorecard entry, first insurer wrap, first non-recourse loan), not a new record. And the first 25-year warranty landing in a building facade rather than a solar farm is the tell that tandem banks on constrained surfaces first.
