## The money is pooling around firm power, not the cleverest device
Step back and one pattern stands out. Data-centre power is the centre of gravity for climate money right now. Climate-tech venture funding rose fifty-five percent in the first half of the year, up to twenty-six billion dollars, and data centres drove that rise.

Look at fusion. It is being funded on grid timelines now, as if it were a power contract. Helion raised four hundred sixty-five million dollars. Focused Energy raised two hundred forty million dollars. That is patient money betting on electricity, not on physics for its own sake.

Look at solar. Tandem solar cells set new efficiency records almost every month. The lab numbers keep climbing. But the bankability gates, the rules that decide whether a bank will finance a project, have not moved. The record and the financeable product are two different things.

Here is the long view. Value moves to whoever locks in firm power and qualified supply. Not to whoever builds the cleverest device. The device gets the headline. The contract gets the returns. Keep your eye on who is signing offtake and who is passing certification, because that is where the money quietly settles.
